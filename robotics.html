Robotics & Autonomous Systems

GuardianX develops AI-powered robotic systems and autonomous intelligence for inspection, safety, residential and commercial environments, and applied research. Our work combines computer vision, decision-making, autonomous control, sensor integration, and human–robot interaction.

Active prototype

GX-01 Home Robotics Research Platform

GX-01 is GuardianX’s experimental quadruped platform for AI perception, autonomous navigation, environmental awareness, voice interaction, and intelligent task execution. The mechanical base is a commercial open-source quadruped. Perception, safety logic, task planning, and the operator interface are developed by GuardianX.

Development areas: Autonomous Navigation · Computer Vision · Environmental Awareness · Voice Interaction · AI Task Planning · Safety Monitoring · Human–Robot Interaction

Status: R&D prototype. Not a consumer product and not offered as a fully autonomous patrol or cleaning system.
